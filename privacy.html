🔒 Zero-Knowledge Privacy Architecture

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026 • Last Updated: October 27, 2026

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Personal Assistant is engineered with a strict zero-knowledge, local-first architecture. Your financial data—including income, expenses, accounts, bank statements, and budgets—belongs exclusively to you.

🛡️ We do not collect, track, store, or sell any of your personal or financial information. There are no tracking SDKs, no behavioral telemetry, no remote analytics servers, and no advertising code inside this application.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

All data generated or imported by you is stored strictly in your device's local sandbox storage using standard W3C IndexedDB (and Web Storage APIs). When running as an Android application, this data is encapsulated in the app's secure, private sandbox directory that cannot be accessed by other installed applications.

3. Optional Google Drive Synchronization

Personal Assistant provides an optional cloud sync feature using Google Drive. When you choose to enable Google Drive Backup:

  • Direct Client-to-API Communication: Sync transactions occur directly between your device and Google's official Drive API over HTTPS. There are no intermediate proxy servers, relay databases, or third-party cloud services.
  • User Scope Access: The app requests only the minimal scope (drive.file) necessary to create, read, and update its own designated backup file (personal_assistant_backup.json). The app cannot read, modify, or access any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Token Handling: OAuth access tokens are held transiently in local storage on your device and are never transmitted to any external server.
  • Disconnection: You can disconnect Google Drive sync at any time with a single tap, which immediately revokes and flushes all local session tokens.

4. Bank Statement Parsing

When you upload bank or credit card statements (CSV or PDF format), all parsing and category extraction is executed 100% locally on your device using client-side JavaScript. Statement files are never transmitted to any server or third-party OCR service.

5. Device Permissions & Network Access

The application requests minimal permissions strictly required for its core functionality:

  • INTERNET: Used solely to communicate directly with Google Drive API (if sync is enabled) and to download progressive web app cache assets.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to monitor online/offline connectivity to manage sync queuing.

The application does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, SMS, or phone storage outside the app sandbox.

6. Data Retention, Export & Complete Deletion

You have full and perpetual ownership of all your data:

  • JSON Backup Export: You can export an unencrypted or encrypted JSON snapshot of your complete ledger at any time.
  • Purge & Factory Reset: You can permanently wipe all accounts, transactions, and categories using the built-in "Purge All Data" tool in Settings. Wiping local data is immediate and irreversible.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling Personal Assistant from your device completely erases all local data stored by the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Personal Assistant is a general audience financial tool and does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected from any user, no children's data is ever gathered or stored.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revision date at the top of this document will be updated. We encourage users to periodically review this page. Any updates will maintain our non-negotiable commitment to zero-knowledge user privacy.
